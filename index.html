Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve? +
We proudly serve Madison and surrounding communities: DeForest, Sun Prairie, Waunakee, Middleton, Fitchburg, Verona, and nearby areas. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
Are your products safe for kids and pets? +
Yes. We use non-toxic, eco-friendly favorites like Dr. Bronner's, Koala Eco, Blueland, Bon Ami, and Branch Basics. Eco-friendly products are always available on request at no additional charge.
How does pricing work? +
Standard cleaning is priced by home size (see our price list above). Deep cleaning adds $50 per room, and add-ons are billed separately. Larger or custom homes always get a free quote.
Do you offer discounts for regular cleanings? +
Yes! Recurring clients save: 15% off weekly, 10% off bi-weekly, and 5% off every four weeks.
How do I pay? +
We accept CashApp, Venmo, cash, and check. Payment is due upon completion of the cleaning.
Do I need to be home? +
Not at all. Many clients provide entry instructions and go about their day. Whatever makes you most comfortable works for us.
